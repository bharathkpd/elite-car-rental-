Common Questions

Everything you need to know about renting with Elite.

What documents are required for rental?
You need a valid Original Driving License, Aadhar Card, and a security deposit. International guests require a Passport and International Driving Permit.
Is there a security deposit?
Yes, a refundable security deposit is required for all vehicles. The amount varies based on the vehicle category (Luxury/Standard).
Are there any mileage limits?
We offer Unlimited Miles for most local rentals. For outstation trips, please confirm with our support team on WhatsApp.
Do you provide home delivery?
Yes, we provide doorstep delivery and pickup across Hyderabad. Charges may apply based on the distance from our hub.
